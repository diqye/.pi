## Fonts

All under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licence texts are in `../fonts/`. Fraunces, Playfair Display, Anton, Bricolage Grotesque and DM Serif Display are from github.com/google/fonts (`ofl/<family>/`, branch main), unmodified. Geist and Geist Mono (Vercel, OFL 1.1) are embedded in `kit/fonts.gen.ts`, as in the other worked films. The licence texts are the `OFL.txt` files google/fonts carries beside each family, unmodified; where a text's copyright year differs from the one inside the font file (Fraunces, DM Serif Display), both are as upstream ships them.

The pictures were checked again against each museum's open-access record on 13 November 2026: all 21 rows are flagged as this page says.
