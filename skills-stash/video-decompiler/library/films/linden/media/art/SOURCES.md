# Linden Library: sources and licences

Every picture in this film is a public-domain work from a museum's open-access collection, taken only where the museum's own record flags it public domain or CC0 (the field and its value are in the last column). Retrieved 12 November 2026. The files here are crops and reductions of those images: each paper cut to a 3:4 sheet and drawn at 600 by 800, the opening sheet also kept large as `hero.jpg`, and each plate drawn 1400 pixels high. Checked again against each museum's open-access record on 13 November 2026: all 29 are flagged as this page says.

## Fonts

Bricolage Grotesque and Fraunces, from github.com/google/fonts (`ofl/<family>/`, branch main), unmodified, SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licence texts are beside them in `../fonts/`. Geist and Geist Mono (Vercel, OFL 1.1) are embedded in `kit/fonts.gen.ts`; their licence texts are `../fonts/OFL-Geist.txt` and `../fonts/OFL-GeistMono.txt`. The licence texts are the `OFL.txt` files google/fonts carries beside each family, unmodified; where a text's copyright year differs from the one inside the font file (Fraunces), both are as upstream ships them.

## Sound

Played in `sound.py` on recorded instruments (`media/sounds/`, each folder with its `LICENCE.txt`); no service, nothing generated. Credit is owed for two of them wherever a film made with them is published:

- Salamander Grand Piano V2 by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), from github.com/Tonejs/audio (`salamander`). Nine MP3 files, as gathered.
- Pizzicato strings from the FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen, rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman (github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Three MP3 files, as gathered.
- Vibraphone and glockenspiel from the Versilian Community Sample Library (github.com/sgossner/VCSL), CC0: no credit needed. The WAV recordings are stored here as FLAC, which is lossless (each decodes to the same samples).
